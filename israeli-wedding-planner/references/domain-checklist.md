# Domain checklist: Israeli wedding planning

Coverage contract for `israeli-wedding-planner`. Generated 2026-08-24 (v1.4.0) from an
expert-persona audit (Religious Council registration clerk + wedding planner); revised
2026-08-25 (v1.5.0), which closed all five Must-cover rows the previous cycle had deferred.
Rows marked DEFERRED are real gaps this cycle did not close; the next update must resolve or
re-justify them.

## Out of scope (explicit)

Re-litigated 2026-08-25; each row still stands.

- **Divorce, get proceedings, get-refusal litigation.** The skill's boundary is the wedding. The pre-chuppah agreements that anticipate divorce are in scope as signposts; the proceedings are not. A user asking this needs a lawyer, not a planning guide.
- **Non-Jewish religious ceremonies inside Israel** (Sharia, Christian denominational, Druze courts). Separate confessional jurisdictions with their own procedure; stated as an anti-trigger in the description.
- **Halachic rulings on an individual's Jewish status, mamzerut or Kohen status.** Signpost to a rav or ITIM only. Emitting a conclusion here would be a personal-status determination. Note the boundary carefully: the berur yahadut PROCESS track, its documents, its appeal window and its exemption are IN scope and are covered as of v1.5.0. What stays out is any conclusion about a given person's status.
- **Immigration advice** beyond signposting the graduated process. The graduated process itself is now described (v1.5.0); what stays out is advice on an individual file, an appeal, or a refusal.
- **Drafting any instrument.** The skill names the pre-chuppah agreements, who publishes them and how they are authenticated. It does not reproduce a form, draft a clause, or advise which form suits a given couple. The same applies to the venue postponement clause: the skill lists what to ask for, a lawyer writes it.
- **Destination weddings abroad as celebrations**, as distinct from civil marriage abroad for registration purposes.
