# Domain checklist: Israeli wedding planning

Coverage contract for `israeli-wedding-planner`. Generated 2026-08-24 (v1.4.0) from an
expert-persona audit (Religious Council registration clerk + wedding planner). Rows marked
DEFERRED are real gaps this cycle did not close; the next update must resolve or re-justify them.

## Out of scope (explicit)

Re-litigated 2026-08-24; each row still stands.

- **Divorce, get proceedings, get-refusal litigation.** The skill's boundary is the wedding. The pre-chuppah agreements that anticipate divorce are in scope as signposts; the proceedings are not. A user asking this needs a lawyer, not a planning guide.
- **Non-Jewish religious ceremonies inside Israel** (Sharia, Christian denominational, Druze courts). Separate confessional jurisdictions with their own procedure; stated as an anti-trigger in the description.
- **Halachic rulings on an individual's Jewish status, mamzerut or Kohen status.** Signpost to a rav or ITIM only. Emitting a conclusion here would be a personal-status determination.
- **Immigration advice** beyond signposting the graduated process.
- **Destination weddings abroad as celebrations**, as distinct from civil marriage abroad for registration purposes.
