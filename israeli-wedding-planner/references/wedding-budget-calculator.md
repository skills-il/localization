# Israeli Wedding Budget Calculator

## Budget Template

Use this template to track wedding expenses. All amounts are in new Israeli shekels.

Every figure printed in this file is an indicative market estimate, not a quoted price. No official body publishes Israeli wedding-vendor prices, and they vary enormously by region, season, day of the week and guest count. Replace each one with a written quote from at least three vendors before you rely on the total.

## Quick Budget Calculator

### Formula
```
Total cost = (per_plate_price x guest_count) + fixed_costs
```

## Matana (Gift) Estimation

### Expected Recovery Rate
Roughly half of the total wedding cost is commonly recovered through matanot. This is a rule of thumb people report, not a measured statistic, so do not commit to spending against it.

| Guest Type | Share of Guests | Avg Gift/Person | Expected Total |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Close family | 10 in 100 | 750 | guests x 0.1 x 750 |
| Friends | 30 in 100 | 400 | guests x 0.3 x 400 |
| Extended family | 25 in 100 | 350 | guests x 0.25 x 350 |
| Colleagues | 20 in 100 | 300 | guests x 0.2 x 300 |
| Acquaintances | 15 in 100 | 250 | guests x 0.15 x 250 |

For 250 guests this worked example yields roughly 87,500 in matanot. Close family and friends tend to give more than the averages above.
