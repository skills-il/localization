## 1.3.0 - 2026-10-01

### Fixed

- Rent figures now come from the CBS average-rent table for Q2 2026 (national NIS 5,085.5) instead of unsourced listing ranges; the unsourced 3.1% yield figure is gone.
- Removed WinWin, which closed in 2020, and the unsupported "English-friendly" label on Homeless.
- Repair remedies now follow the statute: reimbursement after notice (no notice needed for an urgent defect), a proportional rent reduction, and set-off of lease debts against rent (s.25, non-waivable). The 30-day and 3-day repair deadlines are maximums.
- Deposit return runs 60 days from handing the apartment back (or from paying debts, if later), includes any profit earned on the security, and there is no statutory itemised-list duty. An over-cap bank guarantee handed over anyway can still be paid out by the bank.
- Broker fees: a broker is owed a fee only if licensed, holding a signed written order (Brokers Law), and the effective cause of the lease; the fee is negotiated, not a fixed month. A broker may not draft the lease.
- Mamad: since 1992 new construction needs a protected space, either an in-apartment mamad or a floor-level mamak.
- Cash rent is not itself illegal; the troubleshooting now focuses on proof of payment.
- Tel Aviv arnona uses the 2026 municipal order (NIS 46.64-112.99 per sqm for apartments up to 140 sqm).
- The standard lease is a February 2026 draft, not in force.

### Added

- Legal notice, and a "Not legal advice" clause opening the description.
- Fair Rental Law scope (over 3 months or with an option, up to 10 years, rent up to NIS 20,000 CPI-indexed, excluded housing types, the close-relatives exception), the landlord's renewal-notice duty, habitability at delivery, payments a tenant cannot be charged, written-lease contents, option and cancellation notice periods, security draw rules, sublet consent.
- Step 7.6: ownership check (nesach tabu, or ILA / housing-company confirmation), handover protocol, account transfers and arnona objections to the arnona manager, scam checks.
- Glossary: the ground floor is קומת קרקע, a mirpeset shemesh is open to the sky, the Tel Aviv light rail is operating.
- Olim: alternatives to Israeli pay slips and checks, and the Ministry of Construction and Housing rent allowance.
- Broker disclosure duties under the 2024 Ethics Regulations, including the landlord's defects questionnaire.
- `references/tenant-rights-and-move-in.md`, a Reference Links section, and `references/domain-checklist.md`.
- Calculator: `--lease-months` (deposit capped by lease length, and no cap outside the Fair Rental Law), `--broker` (fee only when the tenant signed the order), half rooms, a year-2 oleh note; removed the unsourced "3x income" salary recommendation.
