# Tenant Rights and Move-In Checklist (Israel)

Sourced detail behind Steps 7 to 7.6 of SKILL.md. General information, not legal advice about any specific lease. Statute references are to the Rental and Loan Law 1971 (חוק השכירות והשאילה), sections 25א-25טו, added by the 2017 Fair Rental amendment. Checked 2026-10-01.

## 1. Does the Fair Rental Law apply to this lease?

Section 25טו excludes:

| Excluded | Note |
|----------|------|
| Rent above NIS 20,000 a month | The statute CPI-indexes the threshold every 1 January and rounds it to the nearest multiple of 10 shekels. No updated notice was found at review time, and Kol Zchut still prints 20,000 |
| Leases of 3 months or less with no option to extend | |
| Leases longer than 10 years where the landlord cannot cancel earlier | |
| Hotel and holiday apartments, dormitories (students, pupils, workers), sheltered housing, protected-tenancy apartments | |

Between a landlord and a close relative (spouse, sibling, parent, grandparent, descendant, the spouse's descendant, and their spouses), s.25טו(ב) switches off the non-waiver rule of s.25יד(2), so repairs, forbidden payments, the security cap, and option and cancellation terms can all be varied by contract.

Source: statute s.25טו; Kol Zchut rights pages list the same conditions.

## 2. Habitability at delivery (s.25ו and the First Schedule)

The apartment must have: drainage and sewage removal; electricity and lighting; ventilation openings and natural light with doors and windows to close them, including a lockable main door; drinking-water supply; a partition between the toilet and the apartment; and no unreasonable safety or health risk. Handing over an unfit apartment is a breach even if the lease says otherwise, and the tenant may be released from the lease.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/השכרת_דירה_ראויה_למגורים

## 3. Repairs during the lease (s.25ח, and s.9 remedies)

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Landlord's duty | Fix at their own cost any defect that is not minor and was not caused by unreasonable use |
| Deadline | Reasonable time, no later than 30 days from the tenant's demand; urgent defect (one that prevents reasonable living in the apartment) no later than 3 days |
| Tenant's own duty | The tenant fixes defects caused by their own unreasonable use |
| Remedy if the landlord fails | Repair it and demand reimbursement of reasonable costs, after reasonable advance notice; and, while the defect remains, reduce the rent in proportion to the loss of rental value (s.9(א)-(ב)) |
| Urgent defect | A defect too urgent to wait for the landlord may be repaired at the landlord's expense without a prior demand or notice (s.9(ג)) |
| Set-off | Debts the parties owe each other because of the lease can be set off (s.25), and this cannot be waived in a residential lease. The reimbursement can therefore be offset against rent |

Put the demand in writing (email or WhatsApp with a date) so the 30-day and 3-day clocks can be shown to have started. If you offset a repair cost against rent, do it only once the landlord owes the reimbursement, keep the receipts, and notify the landlord in writing of the set-off: a landlord who disputes the amount may still sue for the unpaid rent.

Sources: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/תיקון_ליקויים_בדירה_מושכרת ; statute ss.9, 25, 25יד at https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה

## 4. Payments a landlord may and may not put on the tenant (s.25ט)

| Tenant may be charged | Tenant may NOT be charged |
|-----------------------|---------------------------|
| Rent | Buying or upgrading fixed systems or installations serving the apartment (except special adjustments the tenant asked for) |
| Arnona and other occupier taxes | Building insurance premiums |
| Utilities (electricity, water, gas) | Payments the landlord owes a third party, including the landlord's broker fee |
| Routine vaad bayit maintenance | Renovations the vaad bayit carries out in the building |

A lease clause that shifts a forbidden payment onto the tenant has no effect even if the tenant signed it.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/תשלומים_שאסור_להטיל_על_שוכר_דירה

## 5. Securities (s.25י)

- Cap on securities that cost the tenant money (bank guarantee, a guarantee from a credit company or insurer, cash): the lower of the rent for 3 months or the rent for 1/3 of the lease period.
- An over-cap bank guarantee or bank check that was handed over anyway stays valid and the bank may pay it in full, so the time to refuse is before signing.
- A regular security check or promissory note that is cashed only on default is not a monetary-outlay security.
- The landlord may draw on a security only for: unpaid rent; defects the tenant was responsible for and did not fix; unpaid current charges (arnona, utilities, vaad bayit); failure to vacate. Reasonable prior notice and a chance to fix are required.
- Return: within 60 days of the apartment being handed back, or of the tenant's debts being paid, whichever is later, including any profit earned on it (for example bank interest).

Sources: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הגבלת_סכום_הערובה_שמותר_לדרוש_משוכר_דירה ; statute s.25י(ג)-(ה) at https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה

## 6. Lease term, option and cancellation (ss.25יב, 25יג)

- Even with no option clause, the landlord must tell the tenant a reasonable time before the term ends whether they want to extend and on what terms (s.25יב(א)).
- Option held by the landlord: at least 90 days' notice before the term ends, and only on terms fixed in the lease in advance. Option held by the tenant: at least 60 days' notice.
- Unilateral cancellation without breach exists only if the lease has a clause for it. A clause letting only the landlord cancel is void unless the tenant has a parallel right. Landlord's notice: at least 90 days (cannot be shortened). Tenant's notice: at least 60 days, or shorter if the lease says so.
- No clause means neither side can end the lease early unless the other breached it. If you may need to leave early (a job change, a first rental after aliyah), negotiate an early-exit or replacement-tenant clause before signing.

Sources: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הארכת_תקופת_השכירות_(אופציה_להארכת_השכירות) ; https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/ביטול_חד-צדדי_של_חוזה_שכירות_שלא_עקב_הפרתו

## 7. The written lease (s.25ב, s.25ג)

In writing, signed by both, a signed copy to each side (an oral or unsigned lease is still valid). Contents include: the address; names, ID numbers and addresses of the parties; the apartment, furniture and fixtures rented, including attached common property; term, option and cancellation terms; rent, payment dates and method; extra payments and their amounts; significant defects or nuisances known to the landlord in the apartment, its surroundings or the building.

If the lease is only in Hebrew, get it translated before signing. You are bound by what you sign.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/חוזה_שכירות_למגורים

## 8. Sublet

Subletting needs the landlord's prior written consent, but if the landlord refuses for unreasonable reasons or sets unreasonable conditions, the tenant may sublet anyway. Whether the refusal was unreasonable is ultimately for a court to decide, so going ahead on your own reading risks a breach claim.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/השכרת_דירה_לדייר_בשכירות_משנה_(סאבלט)

## 9. Brokers

- Conditions for a fee: the broker is licensed, the client signed a written order stating the fee, and the broker was the "effective cause" (הגורם היעיל) that brought about the lease. A broker who was only one of several causes may not be entitled.
- Written order: a broker who did not have the client sign "הזמנה לביצוע פעולת תיווך במקרקעין" is not entitled to a fee.
- The tenant owes the fee agreed in a signed order, even after answering the broker's ad and even when the broker also acts for the landlord. The tenant never pays the fee the LANDLORD owes the broker.
- On request, the broker must show a valid brokerage license.
- Disclosure to a tenant client (2024 Ethics Regulations, in force 9.3.2025): official ownership documents (such as an up-to-date nesach tabu), the size and its source, the extra payments, any personal interest, and a questionnaire the landlord filled in on moisture, building violations, infrastructure defects, a "dangerous building" designation, claims on the property and nuisances.
- A broker may not draft or help draft the lease, or negotiate its legal terms (late-payment penalties, compensation for defects) for the client; a broker who does is not entitled to a fee from that client and commits a disciplinary offense. Negotiating timetables and handover dates is allowed.
- A broker with a personal interest in the deal must tell the client in advance and get the client's prior written consent.
- Complaints go to the Registrar of Real Estate Brokers at the Ministry of Justice.

Sources: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/זכותון_ללקוחות_של_מתווכי_דירות_ומקרקעין ; https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/איסור_גביית_דמי_תיווך_משוכר_דירה

## 10. Before signing and on move-in

| Step | Why |
|------|-----|
| Order a nesach tabu online (gov.il, paid service) | Shows the registered owners, mortgages, liens, court orders and restrictions. If the person renting to you is not the owner, ask why and see the authority. For an apartment not yet registered in the Tabu, ask for the Israel Land Authority or housing-company (חברה משכנת) confirmation |
| Read the lease against sections 4-7 above | Clauses that contradict the law have no effect, but it is easier to strike them before signing |
| Sign a handover protocol at key handover | Condition of each room, appliances, meter readings (water, electricity, gas), dated photos. Repeat at move-out |
| Register as the arnona holder (מחזיק) | The occupier pays arnona, and the oleh discount is requested from the municipality's arnona department. An objection to a wrong bill (wrong zone, type, size or use, or you are not the holder) goes to the arnona manager (מנהל הארנונה) within 90 days of receiving the payment notice, under the Local Authorities (Arnona Appeal) Law 1976, s.3; filing does not suspend payment |
| Move electricity and water accounts into your name, with the handover meter readings | So that usage before your move-in is billed to the previous occupier, not to you |
| Consider contents and third-party insurance | Building insurance covers the structure, not your belongings |

## 11. Scam patterns

- The "landlord abroad": cannot show the apartment, asks for a deposit or a key-courier payment by transfer first.
- A listing copied from another site at a price well below the area's CBS average.
- A tenant presenting themselves as the owner (check the nesach tabu; a sublet needs the owner's consent).
- Pressure to pay a "reservation fee" before a viewing.

Rule: no money before an in-person viewing, a checked nesach tabu and a signed lease.

## 12. Olim-specific points

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| No Israeli pay slips | Offer an employment contract, foreign pay slips, a bank guarantee, or prepaid rent after signing |
| New bank account without checks | Propose a standing order (הוראת קבע) or transfers |
| Arnona discount | Up to 90% on 100 sqm (olim dependent on others' help, receiving a nursing or severe-disability benefit: up to 80% on the whole area), 12 months within the first 24 from registration as an oleh (or from receiving a citizen-oleh certificate); bring teudat zehut with the address appendix and the oleh certificate; if you lived in another municipality first, bring its confirmation that you did not get the discount there. | Rent allowance | Rent support in the absorption basket first, then an automatic Ministry of Construction and Housing allowance. ## 13. Sale of the apartment and paying ahead

- If the landlord sells during the lease, the lease continues against the buyer, and the landlord must notify the tenant in writing (ss.21, 25יא).
- Pay rent ahead only after signing and checking ownership.
