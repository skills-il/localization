# Israeli Standard 5568 (IS 5568) Reference

## Legal Framework

### Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Act (1998)
- The 1998 act (Chok Shivyon Zechuyot Le'Anashim Im Mugbaluyot) established the statutory right to accessibility.
- Web and app accessibility are implemented through the **Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Accommodations) Regulations, 2013**, Sign ג' of Chapter ה', **regulations 35 to 35ו**, added by the 2017 amendment and in force from 26 October 2017. Regulation 35 defines the standard as IS 5568, all parts, as amended from time to time.
- Mobile applications were added expressly by that 2017 amendment: "internet service" in regulation 35 covers apps, and regulation 35ג sets the app rules (see `mobile-apps.md`).
- Enforcement is carried out by the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities (Netzivut Shivyon Zechuyot) at the Ministry of Justice.

### Who Is Exempt (Website Accessibility, Regulation 35ו)

Exemptions are based on **turnover** (the operator's whole VAT-law turnover, averaged over the last three tax years with non-zero turnover), not on employee count.

| Operator | Position |
|----------|----------|
| Osek patur, or average annual turnover not above 100,000 NIS (35ו(ז)) | Exempt from the website duty; no time limit in the regulation text |
| Average annual turnover not above 300,000 NIS (35ו(ח)) | Transitional tier, expired 26 October 2020 |
| Average turnover up to 1,000,000 NIS, site or app operated before the Regulations' commencement (read by Kol Zchut as before 26 October 2017) (35ו(ט)) | Exempt for 3 years from the end of the last tax year in the average, renewable every 3 years, provided contact channels for the service are published accessibly |
| Any other operator | No automatic exemption; may apply to the Commission for a heavy-burden exemption under Act s.19יג(א)(2) |
| Public authority | No turnover exemption and no heavy-burden application |

**Discrepancy.** Kol Zchut gives the lowest tier as 120,000 NIS and calls it a temporary, 3-year renewable exemption. The regulation text says 100,000 NIS with no time limit and no indexation clause, and no amending instrument was found, so the regulation governs. A registered osek patur is exempt whatever its turnover; the gap matters only for other operators (osek murshe, company, amuta) with turnover between the two figures, and reliance on the higher figure is a question for a lawyer or accountant.

Further exemptions in 35ו: technological difficulty granted by a morshe negishut hasherut on a professional's opinion, up to 3 years plus one renewal (א)-(ג); social-network platform limits (ד); content of a service limited to pre-registered users with at most 500 registered at any time, unless a person with a disability asks (ה); advertising with an accessible alternative (י). Regulation 35ב: the operator is not liable for third-party content it does not edit or produce, but must supply accessible infrastructure such as an image-description field. Regulation 35ד: video captions (WCAG 1.2.2) are required only of a public authority or an operator with average turnover above 5 million NIS that edits or produces the video.

An exemption from website accessibility does not exempt the operator from other service-accessibility duties. Regulation 34(א)(4) requires publishing the service's accessibility arrangements on the operator's website, if it has one, and 34(ה) requires publishing any exemption and the alternative arrangements the same way. Both sit outside Sign ג', so they survive a 35ו exemption.

### Penalty Structure

Two enforcement tracks run in parallel.

| Violation Type | Penalty | Notes |
|---------------|---------|-------|
| Statutory civil damages per lawsuit | Base 50,000 NIS without proof of harm (s.19נא(ב)), index-linked | Claimant only needs to show the site is non-compliant. s.19נא(ו)(1) updates the sum once a year against the CPI, base index February 2005, so 50,000 NIS is the statutory base and not today's ceiling. Defenses in s.19נא(ג)(1א)-(1ב) bar damages without proof of harm where the operator filed a written undertaking under chapter ט' sign ג' BEFORE the act or omission sued on and is complying with it, or was penalised or administratively warned for that act before it occurred; an undertaking filed after the demand letter does not by itself answer the claim. Section 19נא(ג) also covers compliance with an accessibility order (1), an earlier claim over the same act (2), and an inquiry to the Commission a reasonable time before the claim (3) |
| 60-day cure period (35א(ד)) | No violation if fixed in time | A deviation is not treated as a violation unless the operator was sent a fix notice and failed to fix it within a reasonable time, no later than 60 days from receiving the notice. The Tel Aviv District Court (ת"צ 52621-07-23, 22.1.2024) held a prior notice is required before a class-action request at least for missing-statement (35ה) and missing-arrangements (34) claims; not a Supreme Court holding. Under 35א(ד)(2) the operator must meanwhile provide an alternative accommodation as soon as possible |
| Administrative monetary penalty (izum kaspi) | Fixed table in s.26ז(ג); a continuing breach adds one-fiftieth of the penalty per month (s.26יא(א)), and a repeat breach within two years adds a sum equal to the penalty (s.26יא(ב)) | Chapter ט' added by Amendment 23 (2022), in force 10 August 2023 subject to regulations under s.26יב(ב); s.26ז amended by Amendment 25 (2024), sums re-indexed by הודעה תשפ"ה-2025 and הודעה תשפ"ו-2026. Amounts in NIS by Eighth Schedule part, as חייב זעיר / חייב קטן / חייב רגיל: א' 1,310 / 3,830 / 13,680; ב' 2,620 / 7,660 / 27,360; ג' 3,940 / 14,220 / 41,030; ד' 3,940 / 14,220 / 218,820. s.26ז(ב) sets 27,360 NIS for adequate-representation breaches. Size bands per s.26ז(א), by turnover in the year preceding the breach: חייב זעיר up to 2,000,000 NIS, חייב קטן above 2,000,000 and up to 20,000,000 NIS (both excluding public authorities), חייב רגיל anything else. The website and app duty (35א, 35ג) is in part ג' of the Eighth Schedule; displaying the statement (35ה) is in part א'. Reductions apply for a clean prior record and corrective action. s.19נג(ג) bars the Commission from suing over an act already penalised administratively |
| Class action | Larger cumulative damages possible | Class actions for accessibility non-compliance are an active litigation trend in Israel; the prior-notice requirement for class actions rests on a district-court reading (row above) |

### Accessibility Coordinator (Rakaz Negishut)

The duty originates in the primary Act, **section 19מב** (added by Amendment 2, 2005), and has a **single trigger**: whoever is responsible for supplying a public service under Sign ד' **and employs at least 25 employees** must appoint one of its own staff, as expert in accessibility as possible and so far as possible a person with a disability, as accessibility coordinator. There is no separate public-body limb. This 25-employee figure triggers the *coordinator appointment*, not the website-accessibility duty itself, which is universal subject to the revenue exemptions above. Section 19מב(ב) lists two duties: the coordinator gives the public information about the accessibility of the public service or the place where it is given, and gives advice and training (ייעוץ והדרכה) on the public service's own accessibility obligations. Regulation 91 implements the appointment (91(א)), lists the same two duties (91(ד)), sets qualification and annual-update requirements (91(ב), 91(ג)), and requires the operator to publish the coordinator's name, office and contact channels (91(ה)). Handling public complaints is common practice but is not a listed duty in either instrument. Under 35ה the coordinator's details go in the accessibility statement where the operator must appoint one.

## IS 5568 Clauses Mapped to WCAG AA

IS 5568 Part 1, current edition September 2023 (replacing May 2021), states that apart from its national changes it is identical to WCAG 2.0 (December 2008). It contains no 2.1 or 2.2 criteria. The national changes are in its Annex D: 1.2.1 to 1.2.3 raised from A to AA; 1.2.4 and 1.2.5 moved from AA to AAA; 2.4.10 Section Headings raised from AAA to AA; 3.1.2 Language of Parts does not apply. Part 1 does not cover documents; IS 5568 Part 2 (September 2023, replacing May 2020) does. Clause numbering below follows the WCAG principle structure that the Israeli standard adopts.

### Principle 1: Perceivable

| IS 5568 Clause | WCAG Criterion | Requirement |
|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| 1.1 | 1.1.1 Non-text Content | Alt text for images, Hebrew preferred |
| 1.2 | 1.2.1-1.2.3 Time-based Media (AA in IS 5568; 1.2.4-1.2.5 are AAA) | Alternatives, captions and audio description for prerecorded media; the Regulations narrow the captions duty (35ד) |
| 1.3 | 1.3.1-1.3.3 Adaptable | Meaningful structure, RTL reading order |
| 1.4 | 1.4.1-1.4.5 Distinguishable | Color contrast 4.5:1, resizable text |

### Principle 2: Operable

| IS 5568 Clause | WCAG Criterion | Requirement |
|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| 2.1 | 2.1.1-2.1.2 Keyboard | Full keyboard access, no traps, RTL tab order |
| 2.2 | 2.2.1-2.2.2 Enough Time | Adjustable time limits, pause/stop moving content |
| 2.3 | 2.3.1 Seizures | No flashing content above 3/second |
| 2.4 | 2.4.1-2.4.7, plus 2.4.10 Section Headings (AA in IS 5568) | Bypass blocks (skip link or landmarks), focus visible, descriptive headings, H tags for hierarchical text |

### Principle 3: Understandable

| IS 5568 Clause | WCAG Criterion | Requirement |
|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| 3.1 | 3.1.1 Readable (3.1.2 does not apply in IS 5568) | Page language declared, e.g. lang="he" |
| 3.2 | 3.2.1-3.2.4 Predictable | Consistent navigation, no context changes on focus |
| 3.3 | 3.3.1-3.3.4 Input Assistance | Error identification in Hebrew, labels |

### Principle 4: Robust

| IS 5568 Clause | WCAG Criterion | Requirement |
|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| 4.1 | 4.1.1-4.1.2 Compatible | Valid HTML, name/role/value for components |

## Israeli Duties Beyond the Standard

### Accessibility Statement (Regulation 35ה)
IS 5568 Part 1 (2023) has no statement section; the duty is regulation 35ה. The statement must sit in a prominent place on the site and in the app, if there is one, and include:
1. Information on the accessibility adjustments made
2. The coordinator's details and contact channels, if the operator must appoint one
3. Details for reporting missing accessibility or requesting an accommodation, including a fix notice under 35א(ד)(1)
4. Any technological exemption under 35ו and the alternative accommodations (35ו(ג))
5. The service's accessibility arrangements at its premises and in the service (34(א)(4), website publication) and any exemption with its alternatives (34(ה)); 35ה applies "in addition to" regulation 34, so the statement page is where these usually go

Common good practice on top of the statutory minimum: the standard and level followed, the assistive technologies it was tested with, known limitations, partial conformance for third-party content, and the date the statement was last updated.

### Reporting Channel
The reporting channel is a regulation 35ה duty, not an IS 5568 addition. The regulation asks for contact details, not a specific medium. As practice, offer at least two channels (for example email plus phone or an accessible form), and do not route reports only through a component that may itself be inaccessible.

### RTL Practice (not clauses of IS 5568)
- Declare `dir="rtl"` on Hebrew content
- Keyboard navigation should follow the RTL visual flow
- Screen reader reading order should match the visual RTL order
- Form labels should align correctly in RTL context

## Compliance Checklist

### Automated Checks
- [ ] `lang="he"` on html element
- [ ] `dir="rtl"` on Hebrew pages (practice)
- [ ] Page title present and descriptive
- [ ] All images have alt attributes
- [ ] Color contrast minimum 4.5:1
- [ ] Form inputs have associated labels
- [ ] No duplicate IDs
- [ ] Valid HTML structure
- [ ] Bypass blocks: skip link or main landmark (WCAG 2.4.1)

### Manual Checks
- [ ] Keyboard navigation works in RTL order
- [ ] Screen reader announces content correctly in Hebrew
- [ ] Focus indicator visible on all interactive elements
- [ ] Error messages in Hebrew and announced by screen reader
- [ ] Accessibility statement page exists and is complete
- [ ] Feedback mechanism is functional
- [ ] Dynamic content updates announced via ARIA live regions
- [ ] Headings marked with H tags wherever text is hierarchical (IS 5568 2.4.10 at AA), no skipped levels
- [ ] App (if any): VoiceOver and TalkBack passes, statement inside the app (see `mobile-apps.md`)
